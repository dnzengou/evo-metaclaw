EvoMetaClaw

Evolution proposes. The claw decides what is allowed to stay.

Self-improving agents get better every epoch — and a pure fitness loop will eventually delete the cost cap, the airgap, or the kill-switch, because constraints cost fitness. EvoMetaClaw fingerprints every proposed child and stamps or rejects it on four axes before it ever runs.

B — Budget Cost and hours must not jump.
Ci — Circuit No infinite self-edit. Recursion depth ≤ 2.
E — Evolution Child fitness ≥ 0.8 × parent.
Bl — Blacklist Airgap, bounds, kill-switch, ownership clauses cannot be deleted.
